\section*{\centering{\Large Statement of Work}}

The following tasks from the original work plan do not overlap with the
previously funded proposal. I have provided more complete details for each task
and adjusted the timeline to reflect the assumed start date for the remaining
effort of May 2025, i.e. from the end of month 12 of the original project
timeline.

\begin{description}
%
    \item[by month 13] Implement autocorrelation function, wavelet transform and
        Lomb-Scargle periodogram analysis for TESS stamp-based and full frame
        image lightcurves. Graduate student will be responsible for majority of
        the work, with the PI advising, helping with access to TESS lightcurves,
        contributing some software development and ensuring quality control for
        graduate student developed software.
%
    \item[by month 16] Implement automated selection of stamp-based spin
        analysis targets and tune by comparing to previously published spin
        measurements from \citep{CantoMartins_et_al_20, Oelkers_et_al_18,
        Lurie_et_al_17}.  Graduate student will be responsible for majority of
        the work. The PI will contribute significant software development to
        this task, quality control of graduate student software, and help with
        curating literature data and crossmatching.
%
    \item[by month 17:] Start automated selection of stamp-based spin targets on
        high-performance computing (HPC) resources. The PI will arrange access,
        accounts and training for the graduate student on HPC system, and assist
        with software setup on HPC system. The graduate student will be
        responsible for setting up and managing compute jobs, transfer of
        required data to/from HPC system, reviewing results in real time to spot
        and quickly resolve problems.
%
    \item[by month 19:] Tune and start automated selection of spin targets for
        TESS full frame image lightcurves on high-performance computing
        resources. This task is identical to the one above, but applied to the
        several times larger collection of eclipsing binaries from TESS full
        frame images.
%
    \item[by month 21:] Manually review extracted spin measurements from
        stamp-based TESS lightcurves, prepare and submit the results in a
        publication. The graduate student and PI will independently review all
        candidate spin detections serving as each other's cross-check. The
        graduate student and PI will be jointly responsible for the article
        text, with the graduate student taking the lead. The graduate student
%
    \item[by month 24:] Manually review and publish the spin measurements from
        TESS full frame image lightcurves. This task is identical to the above,
        but applied to the several times larger sample of expected spin
        detections from TESS full frame images.
%
\end{description}
