\section*{\centering{\Large Scientific and Technical Expertise}}

\vspace{5mm}

\section*{PI: Kaloyan Penev}

Dr. Penev is an expert on high precision photometry and photometric surveys. As
the developer of the HATNet and HATSouth data reduction pipelines and an active
participant in the analysis of photometric data, PI Penev is intimately familiar
with best practices, and potential pitfalls in the analysis underlying the data
that will be used for this project. Furthermore, the PI has a long history of
investigating the effects of tides, which is one of the principle uses of the
proposed catalogues. As such, the PI is in an ideal position to ensure none of
the decisions made for how to carry out the proposed analysis will get in the
way of studying tides and that all analysis products necessaryare provided.

\vspace{5mm}

\section*{Graduat Student: Joshua Schussler}

Mr. Schussler is a Ph.D. student in PI Penev's group at UT Dallas. He is the
origin of the idea for the eclipsing binary physical parameter analysis part of
the proposal, and contributed to the detailed development of the analysis plan
and the demonstration figures presented in the Science/Technical/Management
section. As a result, the student is intimately familiar with the proposed plan
and has the necessary expertise to carry out the proposed analysis. Mr.
Schussler is also involved in the efforts to study tides within the PI's group,
and is currently in charge of a project to use a similar catalog to what we plan
to create, but based on Kepler data to study tides. As a result, the student is
also ideally situated to ensure the highest possible value of the generated
products for tidal analysis.
