\section*{Collaborator: Simon Albrecht, Aarhus Universitet}

Collaborator Albrecht will help advise us during the analysis of photometric and
spectral energy distribution data to extract the physical parameters of
eclipsing binary systems. Dr. Albrecht is an author of a recent study that
carried out that that task for a subset of the data we plan to use in a very
similar manner to what we propose. His participation will dramatically speed up
the process.
